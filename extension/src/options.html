Buddy

Your anti-rug sidekick: open any token on pump.fun, DexScreener, GMGN, Photon, Axiom or Birdeye and it builds the report for you.

Report

Solana RPC (optional)

To read the launch on-chain (bundlers, snipers, dev buy) the buddy uses the public Solana RPC, which gets rate-limited sometimes. Got your own Helius RPC (free plan available)? Paste it here and scans get faster.

Your wallet (read-only, optional)

Paste your Solana wallet address (public, never your seed or keys) and the buddy shows how much you hold of the token you're looking at, and tells you when an alert hits a token you're in. It only reads your balance.

Telegram alerts (optional)

Watchlist alerts on your phone. Create a bot with @BotFather (it gives you a token), send it any message, then paste the token and your chat id (ask @userinfobot for it). The token stays on this PC.

Privacy

  • Never touches your wallet or keys. If you add your wallet address, it's only used to read your balance on the Solana chain.
  • The buddy's memory (tokens it saw and how they ended) stays on this PC.
  • Doesn't screenshot your screen or read the rest of the page.
  • Uses public data only: DexScreener, RugCheck, GoPlus and the Solana chain.
  • The project's website is checked by the Buddy server (it only receives that URL).
  • Open source (AGPL-3.0). Not financial advice.
